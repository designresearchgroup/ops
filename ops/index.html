About Giggy

Giggy is honest job-placement advice from a contracting & freelancing veteran — built to force the job market to tell a real story. The market is noise: ghost jobs, HR spam, aggregator reposts (LENSA and the like), wired postings. Giggy cuts through it with transparency — showing the real cash a role is worth, the honest odds it converts, and the red flags, with its reasoning in the open. Paste your résumé and an opportunity; Giggy gives you the straight read, then drafts a tailored, interview-defensible, ATS/Workday-safe résumé you can save as PDF. Every opinion and every résumé is bound by the honesty guardrails below (HAVE/DON'T, scope-precise, never fabricates).

Static frontend served at jamesecoleman.com/ops/ and shipped to GoDaddy over FTPS on every push to main. The Claude key stays server-side in a Supabase Edge Function (or the GoDaddy PHP proxy); everything you enter stays in this browser. See README.md.
